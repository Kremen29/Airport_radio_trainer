        Tutorial

        Welcome

        Words you will hear

        Delivery
        The controller you call first at the gate. They tell you the runway and the air-pressure setting, then send you to Ground.
        Ground
        The controller who moves aircraft on the taxiways. Taxiways are the roads between the parking stand and the runway.
        Tower
        The controller who looks after the runway. Only Tower can let you go onto the runway or take off.
        Radar
        The controller you call after takeoff. At Prague the call is Praha Radar. They watch aircraft in the air.
        Readback
        You repeat the important parts of an instruction, so the controller knows you heard them. End with your callsign, the aircraft's name on the radio.
        QNH
        The air pressure setting. Pilots put it in the altimeter so height is measured above the sea. Say the digits one by one.
        Squawk
        A four-digit code for the radar box in the aircraft. This practice flight does not use one, so you will not hear it.
        Hold short
        Stop before the runway and stay there. Do not roll onto the runway.
        Line up and wait
        You may go onto the runway and face the takeoff direction. Then stop. It is not permission to take off.
        Cleared for takeoff
        You may start the takeoff roll. This is a separate instruction from line up and wait.
        Super
        A word used on the first call for the largest aircraft, such as the A380. It warns others about strong swirling air behind the aircraft. The score does not require it.
        Phonetic alphabet
        Letters are spoken as words so they are not confused. Alfa, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo, Foxtrot, Golf, Hotel, India, Juliett, Kilo, Lima, Mike, November, Oscar, Papa, Quebec, Romeo, Sierra, Tango, Uniform, Victor, Whiskey, X-ray, Yankee, Zulu.
        Numbers
        Say each digit. Some have a special radio form: 3 is tree, 5 is fife, 9 is niner. The decimal point in a frequency is decimal. Runway 24 is two four, not twenty-four.
